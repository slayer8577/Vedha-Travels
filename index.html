About Vedha Car Rentals

Comfort • Safety • Affordability — Your trusted travel partner in Tirupati

Welcome to Vedha Car Rentals, your trusted travel partner in Tirupati. We specialize in comfortable and reliable car rental services with a variety of packages tailored to meet your travel needs. Whether you're visiting for pilgrimage, business, or leisure, we make your journey smooth, affordable, and memorable.

Our fleet includes Sedans, SUVs, and Tempo Travellers, with options perfect for solo travelers, families, and groups. Every ride is backed by experienced drivers and well-maintained vehicles to ensure comfort and peace of mind.

  • Affordable pricing with no hidden charges
  • Daily rental packages to suit every budget
  • Pilgrimage tour packages — Tirupati Balaji Darshan, Kanipakam, Srikalahasti & more
  • 24/7 customer support for your convenience
  • Clean & sanitized vehicles for your safety
  • On-time pickup & drop — reliable and punctual service
  • Professional & courteous drivers focused on safe driving

Our goal is to make your Tirupati experience hassle-free and memorable. From short city rides to full-day temple tours, Vedha Car Rentals is committed to serving you with dedication and care.
